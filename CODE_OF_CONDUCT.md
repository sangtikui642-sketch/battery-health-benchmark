# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants in Battery Health AutoBench are expected to make collaboration respectful, safe, and
welcoming regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical evidence and decisions without personal attacks.
- Give actionable, specific review feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy, research embargoes, licenses, and data-use restrictions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a technical or governance decision.
- Avoid publishing credentials, personal data, confidential datasets, or exploit details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
credential exposure, bad-faith disruption, plagiarism, fabricated results, and retaliation against
good-faith reporters are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The repository owner, 赵一冰 (`sangtikui642-sketch`), is responsible for clarifying and enforcing
these standards. Proportionate responses may include correction requests, content removal,
temporary participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For ordinary technical disagreements, open a GitHub issue or pull request and keep the discussion
focused on reproducible evidence. For sensitive safety or vulnerability material, use the private
channel in `SECURITY.md`. Abuse involving the repository owner or platform-wide conduct can be
reported through GitHub's reporting and blocking mechanisms:

https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam

Enforcement decisions should protect reporter privacy, preserve necessary evidence, avoid public
retaliation, and state the project rule being applied.

## Attribution

This policy draws on the values and enforcement structure of widely used open-source community
codes while remaining specific to this repository's current maintainer and reporting channels.
